## Forks and modified versions

You are welcome to fork and modify ModBuilder under the license. If you distribute a modified version, including builds, releases or ZIPs:
- give it a different name that does not contain "HD2Runtime" or "HD2R", and replace the reticle icon, wordmark and lockup with your own;
- point `BuildInfo.Repository` (`HD2RuntimeGUI.Core/BuildInfo.cs`) at your own repository, so the in-app updater never installs official releases over your build or offers your build as an official one;
- do not suggest that SkyeShade made, endorses or supports your version.

You may, and under the license must, say where your version comes from, for example "based on HD2Runtime ModBuilder by SkyeShade" with a link to [SkyeShade/HD2Runtime-ModBuilder](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime-ModBuilder). Referring to HD2Runtime by name to say your tool or mod works with it is also fine.

Unmodified copies of the official release ZIP may be shared with their names and icon intact.

For anything not covered here, open an issue on [SkyeShade/HD2Runtime-ModBuilder](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime-ModBuilder/issues).
