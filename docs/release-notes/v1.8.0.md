# HD2Runtime ModBuilder 1.8.0

**HD2Runtime 0.31.0.** ModBuilder now bundles the HD2Runtime 0.31.0 SDK (the published release asset, built at Runtime `2c38305`). Most of what 0.31.0 adds is a new field on a weapon, vehicle or stratagem ModBuilder already edits, so it shows up where you would look for it. The project format does not change.

**Download:** `HD2Runtime-ModBuilder-v1.8.0-win-x64.zip` (Windows 10/11 x64). Extract it and run **`HD2RuntimeModBuilder.exe`**, or use **Install update** from any earlier version.

**New projects bind to [HD2Runtime 0.31.0](https://github.com/SkyeShade/HD2Runtime/releases/tag/v0.31.0).** Projects bound to 0.30.3 (ModBuilder 1.7.1), 0.30.2, 0.30.0 or the 0.30.0-dev test SDK keep their binding and export exactly as before until you **Rebind** them; rebinding keeps every edit. Exports of projects bound to 0.31.0 require HD2Runtime 0.31.0.

## New fields
Most are not tested in game yet. The export writes HD2Runtime's opt-in (`allow_unverified_effect`) for you, and the field shows a warning.
- **Wind-up.** Heat weapons with a firing charge (the LAS-16 Sickle and LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle, the LAS-99 Quasar's warm-up, the AX/LAS-5 Rover gun, the A/LAS-98 Laser Sentry): firing charge, charge gain and loss per second, and whether the charge resets after a shot. The EXO-45 Patriot minigun and the TD-110 Maelstrom gun get wind-up and wind-down times.
- **Beam pulses.** Every beam weapon's beam fire mode, fire rate, beams per pulse and pulse length (the LAS-13 Trident's pulse length is what limits its fire rate).
- **Missiles.** The flight of the missile the StA-X3 W.A.S.P., FAF-14 Spear, MLS-4X Commando, P-33 and P-92 fire: lifetime, speeds, acceleration, turn rates and guidance delay (in the Projectile group).
- **Team-reload ammunition.** The GR-8, RL-77, FAF-14 and StA-X3 can carry spare magazines of their own (0 to 31), and the AC-8 spare rounds (0 to 511). The weapon reloads from them before using the backpack. The five team-reload backpacks get capacity, start and refill amounts; the support weapon's backpack panel says how the two work together.
- **Mounted spread** on the 17 projectile mounts (Exosuit guns, FRV guns, Bastion, Maelstrom and more).
- **Orbital targeting.** The Orbital Railcannon Strike's search radius, movement speed, duration and fire delay, and the Orbital Laser's retarget interval.

## Backpack-fed ammunition rows
- In 1.7.1 the AC-8's and the four launchers' own ammunition rows were read-only, because HD2Runtime 0.30.2 said a value there did nothing. HD2Runtime 0.31.0 makes them the weapons' own spares, so on a 0.31.0 project they are ordinary fields again.
- A project bound to 0.30.2 or 0.30.3 keeps 1.7.1's behaviour until you rebind it. After the rebind, an edit saved on one of these rows is a normal edit, exported with the opt-in 0.31.0 requires.

## Shown read-only for now
- **Beam swaps** (`attack.beam`: the LAS-7 Dagger and other beam weapons firing another catalogued beam), the **Eagle Rearm pool** (`stratagem.rearm_pool`: charges before cooldown) and the **fire-mode selector on single-mode weapons**. These weapons can still switch to a different single mode. All three show their current value; their editors come in a later release.
- **Beam conversion** (experimental) and **status effect stats** (`hd2.status_effect`) have no editor; custom Lua can call them.

## Project format and compatibility
- **No format change.** Projects stay at format 14. Projects bound to an earlier SDK generate the same Lua and export the same ZIPs as with 1.7.1, apart from the ModBuilder version recorded in `build-report.json`.
- ModBuilder 1.7.1 cannot open a project bound to 0.31.0 (it supports SDKs up to 0.30.3).
